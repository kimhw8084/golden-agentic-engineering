# Decision precedence and non-negotiable corrections

Recorded from 107 explicit survey/reconciliation responses (all remain provisional pending formal sign-off). Original detailed records are intentionally omitted from this GitHub-ready ZIP; see `docs/reference/README.md`.

1. Main objective: maximize consistency, code/product performance, delivery speed AND independently verified quality; no composite score.
2. Company internal OpenCode and model primary. Authorized routine dev may edit/refactor/test/commit to main autonomously. Golden imposes no per-edit/commit approval. Production/irreversible operations remain controlled by humans.
3. A single standards system, framework-specific profiles: complex React/TS/Tailwind/shadcn, lightweight NiceGUI, preferred Python/FastAPI if deployment capacity permits. SQLite preferred until a concrete requirement cannot be met.
4. Golden Control holds standards and approved tools; no application registry, app operations, application compliance dashboard or app hosting.
5. Team platform owns actual internal apps, feedback aggregation and unified observability UI. Existing Prometheus/Grafana/Splunk remain monitoring systems.
6. Imported tools require OSI-approved licenses, zero required fees, qualified offline Windows/Linux deployment. Existing approved proprietary company systems may be reused.
7. All rules assessed; execute applicable checks and mark non-applicable evidence. Fast checks local/commit, full on demand/pre-release and after lead-approved standard change with app owner approval to evaluate affected app in CI.
8. Company exceptions preserve original FAIL/NOT_RUN result and approval evidence. Golden-originated repair proposals use existing Git review processes; not a restriction on normal OpenCode edits.
9. Standards releases are approved by team lead after documented testing. Code commits do not automatically deploy applications.
10. Golden must survive OpenCode/AI/control plane outages for essential tests, docs and operations. Versioned local fallback.
11. Every deliverable uses strict black/white/gray, including alarms and scientific data visualizations. Use redundant visual distinctions.
12. App usage feedback: minimal aggregated signals and optional explicit feedback in the existing team platform; only approved standards-relevant summaries reach Golden stewardship.
13. Qualification: new and existing real apps, two pilot engineers, Windows/Linux, model-off, speed, maintainability, users, defects and reproducible results.

Open company decisions remain: exact FastAPI publishing limit, permitted package sources, real CI approval implementation, OSS clearance, metrics thresholds, authoritative waiver roles and detailed team feedback privacy rules.
